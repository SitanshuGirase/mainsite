About Us

Fundamentals-first learning, taught by specialists

The Photon Academy was built on a simple idea: a student learns best when a teacher's full attention is on a single subject. Rather than encouraging rote memorisation, classes are built around clarity — breaking down ideas until they make sense, then practising until they stick.

Every subject, from Class 6th board basics to JEE, NEET and Olympiad-level problem solving, is handled by a dedicated faculty member with a focused command over that subject alone, under the academic direction of Naveen Sachan Sir.

"We don't just finish a syllabus — we build the habit of understanding, so it stays long after the exam is over."

— Naveen Sachan Sir, Director

Naveen Sachan Sir

Head & Director · Social Studies & English Faculty

Naveen Sachan Sir leads The Photon Academy and personally teaches Social Studies and English, overseeing the academy's approach to concept-based, fundamentals-first learning across every batch.

"A strong foundation in Class 6th makes Class 12th — and every competitive exam after it — far less intimidating."

Privacy Policy

The Photon Academy, Ankleshwar, Gujarat, India · Last updated: 5 October 2026

This page explains what information we collect on our website, why, and how it is protected. It covers our public forms (Admission, Scholarship, Contact) and the Student Space.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in (Student Space): your name and email address, as provided by your Google account. We never receive your Google password.
  • Student profile: class, board, school, date of birth, parent or guardian name and phone number, your photo, and optionally your own phone number and address.
  • Academy records you can see: tests, homework, class timetable and notes added by the academy for your class or for you.
  • Website forms: whatever a visitor types into the Admission, Scholarship or Contact forms, including any photo they upload.

Why we use it

  • To check that a student is real before approving their account.
  • To show each student their own schedule, timetable and notes.
  • To keep academy records up to date and to contact parents or guardians about the student.
  • To respond to admission, scholarship and contact enquiries.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only the academy’s administrators (the director and authorised staff) can see student profiles and form submissions. A student can see only their own profile and the schedule items addressed to their class or to them. Photos are stored in a private area and are not public.

Services that help us run the website

  • Supabase stores our database, files and sign-in records (data region: Mumbai, India).
  • Google provides the sign-in.
  • An AI service (Groq) powers the study assistant. When a student asks the assistant something, it receives the question, the student’s first name, class and board, and the student’s test, homework and timetable entries. It does not receive photos, phone numbers, addresses, dates of birth or parent details.
  • Telegram and email notify the academy that a new form was submitted. These alerts contain only the form name, the applicant’s name, their class and a link to the protected admin page.

Children

Many of our students are under 18. A parent or guardian may ask to see, correct or delete their child’s information at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep student information while the student is part of the academy. We delete it on request, or when it is no longer needed for academy records.

Your choices

  • Students can update their profile details in Student Space at any time.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete information by contacting the academy through the Contact page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
